A LITTLE NOTE ♡

A Letter From My Heart

✦

Dear Bhai,

Some relationships are difficult to describe in words because they are simply too special.

There are so many things I feel for you that I don’t always know how to put them into words. I may not talk to you as often as I would like to, and sometimes I even hesitate while talking because I don’t know what to say. But please never think that the distance or less communication means I care any less.

I love you all so much, and I miss you more than I can say. Even when we don’t talk for a long time, you are always somewhere in my thoughts and always have a special place in my heart. Some bonds don’t need everyday conversations to remain strong—they simply stay close to the heart. ❤️

I wish I could spend more time with you, share more little moments, and talk without any hesitation. Maybe I don’t always express it, but having you as my brothers is truly one of the most precious blessings in my life.

I always pray to God to bless you with success, happiness, good health, and endless opportunities. May you achieve everything you dream of, keep growing in life, and always have a reason to smile. No matter where life takes you, I hope you always stay happy, safe, and surrounded by good people.

And if I ever fail to say it enough, just remember one thing—you all mean more to me than my words can ever explain. ❤️

Happy Raksha Bandhan, My Brothers! ❤️

With all my love, Your Sister ♡
