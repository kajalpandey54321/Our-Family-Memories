Dear Khushboo Didi

🌸The Love Behind the Anger
You somehow always manage to look like you’re angry with me. 😂❤️ And whenever you’re in the kitchen, I know I’m definitely not allowed to enter! If you make something for yourself, you’ll happily enjoy it, but the moment I ask for some, I’m the one who gets a lecture. 😂 But behind all that anger and our endless little fights, there is a lot of love and care. You share your things with me, I proudly wear your clothes, and whenever you go somewhere, you make sure to take me along. ❤️ Our silly arguments, teasing, and little moments are what make our bond so special. **Thank you for being the perfect combination of a little strict, a little dramatic, and a lot caring.** 😂❤️
**With lots of love,**
**Your sister, Kajal 🤍**

With Love, Kajal ♡
